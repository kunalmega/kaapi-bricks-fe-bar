## 1. Invoice reconciliation against a real PO

**Input:** `sample-invoices/invoice_mysore_po01057.pdf`, generated by `scripts/make_demo_invoice.py`
against **PO-01057**, a real pending PO in the current dataset. Discrepancies were planted on purpose,
so the correct answer was known before the run.

**The PO (queried from silver `po_line_items`):**

| PO-01057 · Mysore Sweet Works (SUP-006) → Koramangala (STR-001) · pending | qty | unit price | line total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Badam Paste (ING-014) | 2.15 kg | ₹800 | ₹1,720.00 |
| Rose Syrup (ING-013) | 6.39 L | ₹200 | ₹1,278.00 |
| Jaggery (ING-005) | 12.69 kg | ₹80 | ₹1,015.20 |
| Palm Jaggery (ING-018) | 5.76 kg | ₹150 | ₹864.00 |

**Extracted by the model (actual `extracted` field):**
```json
{"supplier_name": "MYSORE SWEET WORKS", "invoice_number": "MSW-INV-2026-0729",
 "po_reference": "PO-01057", "subtotal": 4534.7, "gst_amount": 226.74, "total_amount": 4761.44,
 "items": [["Badam Paste", 2.15, 800.0], ["Rose Syrup", 6.39, 230.0],
           ["Jaggery Blocks", 14.0, 80.0], ["Sugar", 5.0, 45.0]]}
```
Header fields and all 4 line items match the source PDF exactly.

**Reconciliation output (actual `matched_items`), compared with the planted answer:**

| Line | App status | App message | Planted | Caught? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Badam Paste | match | — | match | ✅ |
| Rose Syrup | error | Price: contract ₹200.0, invoiced ₹230.0 (+₹30) | price overcharge | ✅ |
| Jaggery Blocks | warning | Qty: ordered 12.69, invoiced 14.0 (+1.3) | quantity over-bill | ✅ |
| Sugar | extra | Not in PO — extra item | unordered item | ✅ |
| Palm Jaggery | missing | In PO but not in invoice — missing delivery | short delivery | ✅ |

**5 of 5 planted discrepancies caught.** Money at stake on this one invoice:

| Exception | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---:|
| Rose Syrup overcharge | 6.39 L × ₹30 | ₹191.70 |
| Jaggery over-bill | 1.31 kg × ₹80 | ₹104.80 |
| Unordered Sugar | 5 kg × ₹45 | ₹225.00 |
| **Billed but not owed (pre-GST)** | | **₹521.50** |
| Palm Jaggery not delivered (stock gap, not billed) | 5.76 kg × ₹150 | ₹864.00 |

**Two defects found and fixed by this capture:**
1. *Missing-delivery check never fired.* It looked up the key `invoice_name` on the extracted
   items, which use the key `name`. The lookup returned `""`, and `"" in "palm jaggery"` is `True`,
   so every PO line counted as delivered. It now compares against the PO line each invoice item
   actually matched. The first run (13:50:46 UTC) caught 4/5; after the fix, 5/5.
2. *No supplier check on the PO reference.* See case 2.

Known limitation: `purchase_orders.total_amount` (₹1,400.50 for PO-01057) is generated
independently of the line items (₹4,877.20). Reconciliation therefore works at line level only; it
does not compare header totals.

## 2. Stale PO reference (regression case)

**Input:** `sample-invoices/invoice_coorg_mixed.pdf`, an older sample that cites **PO-01288**.
After the data was regenerated, PO-01288 belongs to a different supplier (Mysore Sweet Works, STR-027).

- **Before the fix (13:50 UTC):** the app matched PO-01288 anyway and reported every line as an
  "extra item", which gave the manager a misleading reconciliation.
- **After the fix (13:51:10 UTC), actual `warnings`:**
  `"PO PO-01288 belongs to SUP-006, not invoice supplier SUP-001 (Coorg Coffee Estates) — reference ignored"`.
  The app then fell back to Coorg's open PO for the store, **PO-01314**, and reported
  `Coorg Arabica Beans — Grade AA: Qty: ordered 41.95, invoiced 25.0 (-17.0)`. The other 4 lines
  were correctly flagged as not on that PO.
